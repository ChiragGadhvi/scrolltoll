# 🛡️ Rotto - Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-08-21

Rotto is a screen time tracking tool designed to prioritize user privacy above everything else.

---

### 1. 🔒 100% Local Processing
Everything Rotto does happens on your physical device. We do not use any external servers, cloud databases, or tracking scripts. Your screen time data is used ONLY within the app, to add up how long you spend in the apps you choose to track and show Rotto's score and mood for the day.

### 2. 📵 No Data Collection
- **We do NOT collect** your name, email, or any personal identity information.
- **We do NOT read** your app content, messages, or keystrokes.
- **We do NOT send** your app usage metrics to any third parties, including our own team.

### 3. 📊 What Permissions We Use
To function correctly, Rotto requires the **Usage Access** permission. This is a standard Android permission that allows the app to see how many minutes you spent on other apps. This permission is handled entirely on-device and is never shared externally.

### 4. 🗄️ Database Storage
We use the **Hive** storage engine locally on your phone to store your Rotto history so you can see your own progress. It sits in the app's private storage, which Android keeps isolated from other apps and covers with device encryption on any modern phone. If you delete the app, this data is wiped from your device.

### 5. 🧠 Not a Medical Product
Rotto's score and moods are for entertainment purposes only. They are not a diagnosis of addiction, mental illness, or any medical condition, and should not be treated as medical or psychological advice.

### 6. 📧 Contact Us
If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer via the official Google Play developer page.

---

**Summary:** Your data never leaves your pocket.
